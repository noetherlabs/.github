# Noether Labs

**Adaptive optics, system identification & reliable control.**

We build research software for precision optical instruments: differentiable wavefront models, active calibration and reproducible experiments in closed-loop control.

### Research

| Project | Work |
| --- | --- |
| [Noether Replay](https://github.com/noetherlabs/noether-replay) | Offline telemetry reconstruction; timing, calibration and command-provenance qualification. |
| [Noether AO](https://github.com/noetherlabs/noether-ao) | Wavefront propagation, predictive and learned controllers, fault experiments. |
| [ProbeBench](https://github.com/noetherlabs/probebench) | Phase diversity, sequential calibration and measurement selection under photon budgets. |

Start with [Replay](https://github.com/noetherlabs/noether-replay) to inspect an instrument recording, [ProbeBench](https://github.com/noetherlabs/probebench) to study measurement choices under a photon budget, or [Noether AO](https://github.com/noetherlabs/noether-ao) to examine simulated control and recovery experiments.

Reports retain assumptions, raw evidence and negative results. Telemetry consistency checks and simulator studies do not establish hardware performance or safe autonomous operation.
